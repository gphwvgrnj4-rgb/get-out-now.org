Get Out Now
About How It Works FAQ Contact Privacy Terms

Get Out Now helps Jews eligible under Israel's Law of Return (1950) apply for free point-to-point flights. Eligibility is screened in coordination with official agencies; final aliyah decisions rest with the relevant Israeli authorities. Funding is currently pending, so tickets are awarded first-come, first-served. This project does not speak on behalf of the Government of Israel or the Jewish Agency.
